Ollama listens beyond loopback

Review · Observed

Evidence: DEMO: recognized Ollama process on TCP 0.0.0.0:11434.

The listening address is not restricted to the local loopback interface.

Unknowns: Reachability from another device, firewall rules, internet exposure and authentication were not tested.

Next step: If remote access is unnecessary, ask the service owner to restrict the listening address to loopback. Otherwise review firewall restrictions and authentication. Do not change a shared service without its owner.

Verification: Run this check again and confirm the intended binding. Ask IT to validate required remote access separately.
